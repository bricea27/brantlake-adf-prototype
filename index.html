In Memoriam

Andrew Dreyer

A Brant Laker, remembered by the brothers who knew him.

Andrew spent his summers on the shores of Brant Lake and carried what he found here — the friendships, the lake at dawn, the easy belonging — with him for the rest of his life. When he was gone, the alums who loved him decided that other boys should have that summer too.

More than four decades later, the Fund they started in his name is still doing exactly that.

How the Fund Works
